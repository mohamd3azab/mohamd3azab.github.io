Why physical models

A model provides a different way to understand scale, massing, movement and material relationships. It is a practical communication tool when the purpose and required detail are clear.

Production approach

This work involves reviewing source geometry, choosing an appropriate scale, organizing components for cutting or printing, selecting materials and planning assembly. Techniques can include CNC preparation, laser cutting, acrylic work and 3D-printed elements.

Scope and evidence

Only completed and approved work should be shown as finished. Ongoing client proposals, budgets or confidential designs are not public portfolio deliverables.

Attribution & documentation. Independent commissions and collaborative fabrication workflows. Original drawings, rendered views and verified project documentation will be added as they are curated and cleared for publication.
