Making architecture tangible

Architectural models help explain the form, spatial organization and technical character of complex projects at a scale that can be viewed and understood directly.

The process

The work can involve preparing digital models for cutting, planning material assemblies, laser or CNC-oriented workflows, model construction, lighting and physical detailing.
