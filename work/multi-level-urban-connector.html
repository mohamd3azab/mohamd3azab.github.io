The Multi-Level Urban Connector

An academic exploration of how layered spaces can reconnect urban life across a difficult topography.

The question

The graduation project responds to a central question: how can architectural space help rebuild forms of human connection that are weakened by urban separation?

The spatial strategy

The proposal works with several levels of connection and public activity. Create, Play and Gather organize the project as complementary kinds of urban experience, linking movement and occupation.

Architectural development

Design development considers the relationship between circulation, public programming, built form and the steep urban context. The project was completed as part of the Bachelor of Architecture at The Hashemite University.
