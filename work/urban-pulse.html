Recognition / Team achievement

First Place — ELMA 2026

Urban Pulse, the proposal for Jabal Al-Zuhour Stairs, received first place in the Environmental Legacy Makers Award. Mohammad Azab participated as part of the winning team.

His public LinkedIn update describes receiving the award at Ras El Ain Hangar in Amman.

LinkedIn profile & public updates ↗

A collaborative achievement. The public update credits Raghad El-Sheikh, Razan Al-amad, Ahmad Alhadidii and Nermin Alkhatib alongside Mohammad Azab.
