Urban Pulse — Jabal Al-Zuhour

A site-specific urban intervention developed for the everyday experience of a public stairway in Amman.

Type
Urban intervention / Competition
Location

Year

Role / Result

Project / Practice
Selected record

The context

Jabal Al-Zuhour's stairs are part of the city's everyday pedestrian landscape. Urban Pulse began with the question of how an existing passage through the city might support a richer public-space experience.

The approach

The proposal was developed as an urban intervention concerned with the relationship between place, movement, local use and environmental improvement. The design work was prepared for the Environmental Legacy Makers Award (ELMA).

The outcome

Urban Pulse received first place in the 2026 Environmental Legacy Makers competition. This recognition belongs to the project team rather than to an individual author.

Attribution & documentation. Project developed with Raghd Al-Sheikh, Razan Al-Amad and Nermin Al-Khateeb. Original drawings, rendered views and verified project documentation will be added as they are curated and cleared for publication.
