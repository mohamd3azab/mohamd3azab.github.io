Zarqa Public Art Installation

Public-art design that transforms local urban references into layered silhouettes and fabrication-oriented forms.

Project / Practice
Selected record

Local references

The project investigates how recognizable elements of Zarqa's urban memory can be represented through abstract graphic silhouettes, rather than literal miniature reproductions.

Design system

Three parallel rails organize a layered sequence of forms. The design work includes studying the outlines of different local landmarks and translating them into geometry suitable for CNC and other fabrication processes.

Project status

The work is presented here as design and fabrication development. This page does not claim that the installation has been constructed.
