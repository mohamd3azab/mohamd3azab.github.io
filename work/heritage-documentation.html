Heritage / Digital Documentation

Documentation purpose

Photographic documentation and photogrammetry support the recording of existing heritage fabric, including geometry, material traces and surviving ornament.

Method

The portfolio contains site photographs and digital 3D reconstructions associated with documentation work in Petra and Amman. Agisoft Metashape was part of the documented training workflow.

Professional context

Heritage documentation is presented separately from original architectural design, as it requires careful attribution and respect for the existing site and collaborating institutions.

Some work is associated with training through Sela for Training and Protection of Heritage. Images show a documentation process, not an original building design. These photographs are sourced from the 2026–2027 architecture portfolio supplied for the website.
