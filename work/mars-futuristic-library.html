Mars — Futuristic Library

A sculptural public-library concept exploring a continuous, flowing architectural form and an immersive interior experience.

Type
Public Library / Academic
Location
Abdoun · Amman, Jordan
Year
2024
Role
Academic design
Selected Project
Public Library / Academic

Concept

The Mars Futuristic Library studies an expressive architectural form organized around fluid movement, integrated interior and exterior space, and contemporary public use.

Spatial experience

Curved surfaces and continuous transitions shape circulation through the building. The portfolio also includes sections, renderings and performance-study graphics.

Design communication

The project is presented through concept diagrams, interior views and exterior architectural visualization.

The project is an academic proposal; the imagery represents design work, not a completed building. Project imagery is available in the extended published portfolio.
