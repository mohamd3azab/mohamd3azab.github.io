Amman Spectrum Media City

A high-rise architectural proposal organized around two towers and a multi-podium campus.

The brief

The academic project explores the scale and spatial relationships of a media-city development in Amman.

The composition

The proposed scheme uses a pair of towers and three L-shaped podium volumes, addressing massing, campus organization, relationships between parts and overall architectural identity.

Design focus

The project served as a vehicle for architectural representation, high-rise composition and complex-program organization.
