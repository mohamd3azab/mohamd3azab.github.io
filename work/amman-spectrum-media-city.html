The architectural proposal

Spectrum develops a media-oriented mixed-use complex through twin towers, a layered podium and shared program spaces.

Design process

The portfolio presents massing and concept evolution, plans, sections, program diagrams, exterior renderings and a physical study model.

Spatial organization

A linked podium and vertical program distribution connect activity across different levels, while the towers establish a strong architectural presence.
