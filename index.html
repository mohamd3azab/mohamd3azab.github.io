03 / MAKAN Thinking Entity

Architecture is also a way of reading the city.

MAKAN is an independent platform for urban and architectural thinking — focused on everyday life, context, development and the forces that shape cities.

The platform studies architecture beyond form: how people move, how public space performs, how development decisions affect daily life, and how technology can support better judgement instead of becoming the objective itself.
