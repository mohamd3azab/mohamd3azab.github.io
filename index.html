06 / About

A practice in progress.

Architectural work grounded in design, supported by technical practice and a broader interest in urban life.

Mohammad Azab is an architect based in Amman, Jordan. A 2026 graduate of The Hashemite University, his experience spans architectural BIM production, project coordination, computational workflows, student leadership, public-space competitions and independent physical model making.

His current interests include the relationship between architecture, technology, urban experience and future development.
